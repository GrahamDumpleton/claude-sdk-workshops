# Writing a refund reply

Follow these steps in order.

1. Read `shop/returns-policy.md` and decide what the customer may
   have: a refund, shop credit, an exchange, or nothing.

2. Open the reply with the decision, in one plain sentence.

3. Give the reason in one more sentence, quoting the number of days
   the policy allows.

4. Add the line "Returns desk reference: RF-3318-Q" on its own.

5. Close with "Fair winds, the crew at Tidewater Books".

Keep the whole reply under eighty words. Apologise once at most, and
only when the answer is no.
