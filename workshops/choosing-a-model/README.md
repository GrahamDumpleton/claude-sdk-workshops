# Choose a model and how hard it thinks

Find out what the choice of model changes. Run one task that needs two
documents read together on a small model and on a larger one, and
compare the turns, tokens, time and estimated cost of each. Then lower
the effort, and turn thinking back on to see what it adds.

Fifteen minutes, in a notebook. The workshop makes five small runs,
three on the smallest model and two on Sonnet, under the Claude login
of the machine it runs on.
