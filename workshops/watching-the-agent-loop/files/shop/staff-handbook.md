# Tidewater Books: staff handbook

## Opening up

Unlock the side door first. The alarm panel is behind the poetry
shelves and gives you 40 seconds.

## The till

Count the float at the start of the day. It should hold $160.

## Returns without a receipt

The returns policy says what the customer may have. This is what you
do:

1. Check the book carries our price sticker. No sticker, no return.
2. Write the return in the green ledger kept under the till: the date,
   the title and the customer's name.
3. Issue the shop credit as a paper credit note. A credit note is good
   for 11 months.
4. Credit of more than $45 needs the duty manager's initials in the
   ledger before the note is handed over.

## Special orders

A book we do not stock can be ordered for a customer. Take a deposit
of one fifth of the price.

## Closing

Cash up, set the alarm, and leave by the side door.
