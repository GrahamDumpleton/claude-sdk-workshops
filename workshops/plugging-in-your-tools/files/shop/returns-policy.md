# Tidewater Books: returns policy

What we tell customers.

- **With a receipt.** A book in the condition it was sold in can be
  returned within 23 days of the date on the receipt, for a refund
  paid the way the book was paid for.

- **Without a receipt.** No refund is paid. A book that still carries
  our price sticker can be exchanged, or returned for shop credit at
  the price on the sticker.

- **Never returnable.** Magazines, newspapers, and books bought from
  the sale table.

Staff: the staff handbook says how a return without a receipt is
recorded and when it needs a manager.
