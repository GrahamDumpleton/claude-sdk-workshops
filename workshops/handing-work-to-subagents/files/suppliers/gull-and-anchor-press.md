# Gull and Anchor Press: terms of supply

Orders received before two in the afternoon are picked the same day.
Orders received later are picked on the following working day.

Damaged deliveries must be reported within two working days, with a
photograph of the outer packaging and of the damaged copies. We replace
damaged copies without charge.

Orders may be placed by telephone, by post or through our trade website.
An order placed through the website is confirmed by email within the
hour.

Gull and Anchor Press supplies poetry and short fiction from writers of
this coast. Our list this season runs to several hundred titles, and the
full catalogue is sent to account holders each spring and autumn.

We do not substitute editions without asking. Where a title is
reprinting, the order is held and you are told the date it is expected.

Prices are those ruling on the day of dispatch. They are subject to
change without notice, though we give notice wherever we can.

A trade discount of 35 per cent off the published price applies to every
title, with a further two per cent on orders of more than fifty copies.

Delivery is normally made within 9 working days of an order being
placed. The minimum order is 6 copies, which may be of mixed titles.
Carriage is charged at cost on every order.

Our representative for the region is Honor Tregear, who visits shops
each spring and autumn and is glad to arrange a visit at other times by
appointment.

Titles may be returned in saleable condition within 6 months of the
invoice date, for credit against future orders. Titles marked as firm
sale on the invoice cannot be returned.

Shelf talkers, window posters and other display material are available
on request for the lead titles of each season, without charge to account
holders.

Accounts are settled monthly. Statements are sent on the tenth working
day of the month, and payment is due within thirty days of the statement
date.

New accounts are opened on receipt of two trade references. The first
order on a new account is supplied against payment in advance.
