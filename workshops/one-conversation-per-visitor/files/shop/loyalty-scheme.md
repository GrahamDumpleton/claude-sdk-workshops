# Tidewater Books: the Tide Card

The Tide Card is our loyalty scheme. It is free to join.

- **Points.** One point for every dollar spent on books. 150 points
  become a $10 credit note.

- **Longer returns.** A member has 37 days to return a book with a
  receipt, not the usual number. A return made after the usual number
  of days is paid as shop credit, not as a refund.

- **Points on returns.** The points earned on a returned book are
  taken off the card.

- **Events.** Members book author evening tickets two days before
  anyone else.
